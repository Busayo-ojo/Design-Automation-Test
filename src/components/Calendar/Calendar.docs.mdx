### Interactive

`InteractiveDateSelector` adds the behaviour layer on top — single and range selection, month navigation, and the Month & Year picker.

<Canvas of={CalendarStories.InteractivePlayground} />

**Range selection.** The first click arms the start, the second closes the range, a third starts over. The ring lands on the two endpoints only; everything between fills — exactly the `Multi-Selected` positions from the Figma `Date` set.

<Canvas of={CalendarStories.InteractiveRange} />

**Month & Year selection.** Click the month-year label to open the picker, choose from the Chips, then press Done to return to the grid on the chosen month. The section chevrons page through months four at a time and years five at a time, as Figma shows them.

## Notes

- **Reuse.** The CTAs are the existing `Button` element (text "Cancel" + primary "Done", both `size="lg"`, 142px). The month and year pills are the existing `Chip` element (`Type=Input`, `default` / `focused`). Every icon comes from `foundations/icons`, linear per the directional-icon rule.
- **Tokens.** Every colour mapped to an existing token except two, which were added: `--color-date-cell-disabled` (`#F7F9FC`) and `--color-date-section-label` (`#666666`).
- **Variant differences.** `Date Picker + Last Day` hides the header, closes the column gap to 0, ends on a double-width "Last Day" cell, and carries **no CTAs at all**. `Month & Year Picker` hides the weekday row and swaps the grid for the chip sections.
- **Scale artifact.** Figma's inspector reports 9.52px padding and 0.95px strokes because the set is scaled by 0.9517. The rendered geometry — 38×38 cells, 10px radius, 1px borders — is what the CSS targets.
- **Known Figma data error.** The reference artwork draws July 2026 starting on a **Saturday** (five leading days). July 1 2026 is actually a **Wednesday**, so the grid here shows two. The component computes real dates; the structure, spacing and styling still match Figma exactly. Worth correcting in the Figma file.

## Usage

Reach for `Date Picker` when a specific date or range is needed, `Date Picker + Last Day` where a period can be closed on its final day, and `Month & Year Picker` to jump the calendar to a different month or year — which is how Figma describes it: *"Users can switch to a different month or year in the calendar in case they need to make modifications."*
